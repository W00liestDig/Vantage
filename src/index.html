The 3 nearest hilltop viewpoints you can drive to

      How viewpoints are chosen
      • Hill: summit with ≥ 30 m topographic prominence (TuMP; HuMP ≥ 100 m, Marilyn ≥ 150 m).
      • Relative elevation: height above the lowest 10% of land within 3 km – not sea level.
      • Viewpoint: ≥ 30 m relative elevation, ≤ 50 m walk from a public drivable road or car park, ≥ 25% of the horizon open.
      • Drivable: public OSM roads and sealed tracks; motorways, private, driveways and farm/forestry tracks excluded.
      • View: land cover (Clearing, Farm, Urban, Forest) × terrain openness. Sunrise/sunset lines checked up to 30 km.
